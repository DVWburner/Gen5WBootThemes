**Gen5W Boot Themes** lets you customize your head unit's **Boot Image** (the static logo shown during cold boot) and **Goodbye / Welcome Video** (the start/stop MP4 animation), mix-and-match parts from different themes, browse and download themes in-car from **[https://market.ioniqthemes.com/](https://market.ioniqthemes.com/)**, or create and publish your own themes using **Boot Theme Studio** for Windows.

> [!NOTE]
> **Normal Head-Unit Behavior (Boot Image & Goodbye Video):**
> Your **Boot Image** and **Goodbye Video** do **not** play on every single startup and shut down — for example, quick sleep/wake or short ignition cycles skip them. **This is true of the factory stock system as well, and is not a bug in this software.**

## How It Works & Safety

- **Zero `/system` Disk Modifications:** Goodbye/Welcome videos (`com.mobis.welcome`) are applied via a RAM bind-mount (`bootthemes-helper.sh`) while leaving `/system` untouched on disk.
- **Hardware-Verified Splash Flashing:** Custom Boot Images are encoded to Telechips `TCC_JPU_C6`-compliant `Baseline YCbCr 4:4:4` JPEGs, packed into the 16 MB `splash` layout (`mmcblk0p7` & `mmcblk0p20`), and `cmp`-verified after flashing. Your original factory splash is backed up to `/data/nemesis/boot_themes/splash_stock.img` beforehand.
- **Emergency USB Kill-Switch:** Placing an empty file named `RESTORE_STOCK.txt` (or `RESTORE_BOOT_THEMES.txt`) in the root of a USB drive (or `/BootThemes/`) and plugging it into the car automatically unmounts custom videos and flashes back your factory stock Boot Image.

## Building From Source (Windows)

```powershell
# 1. Build, sign, and package the in-car APK & NEMESIS USB bundle
python ".\tools\build_bootthemes_apk_and_pkg.py"

# 2. Publish & self-test BootThemeStudio (.NET 10 SDK)
dotnet publish ".\studio\BootThemeStudio\BootThemeStudio.csproj" -c Release -o ".\dist\BootThemeStudio"
```
